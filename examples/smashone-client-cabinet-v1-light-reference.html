Design decisions

  • Aibro mirrorSidebar 240 + workspace card + glass topbar + 6-tile KPI grid + activity feed left / widgets right. Same skeleton, SmashOne body — Vivid Sovereign gold (not emerald), Onest display (not mixed pair), and Mary's verbatim shop data.
  • 60-30-10 goldGold appears only where it sells: AI Assistant tile mark + progress fill, sidebar Home indicator, “Quick post” CTA, PRO chip border, time-of-day glow. Everywhere else is neutral grayscale + semantic states.
  • Authentic dataKaren Mills $2,850 Eastlake mahogany dining table, Sarah Patel viewing Sat May 23, @charlotte_decorator IG DM, low-stock “Antique brass candlesticks pair” — sourced from Mary's High Cotton Antiques DEMOBIZ.
  • Typography disciplineOnest 700 / -0.022em for the H1 greeting and KPI big numbers; IBM Plex Mono with tabular-nums on every numeric field (quotas, prices, timestamps, the kbd chip); Inter 450 body; sentence case throughout.
  • Accessibility AAA on metricsKPI numbers and activity lead lines clear 7:1 against white. Status pills triple-encode icon + color + label. Visible 2px gold focus ring with 4px offset, ⌘K palette discoverable, 44px primary CTA hit target.
  • 6 platforms — canonicalWave 1 (FB · IG · TG · AI) live in the platforms tile and feed. Wave 2 (WA · TT · GBP) are deliberately absent from this concept — they ship as Coming July 2026 placeholders on the Platforms surface, not Home.
  • Microscopic polishHairline borders rgba(0,0,0,0.04–0.06), `0 1px 2px + 0 8px 24px` two-step card shadow, 14 px radius cards / 10 px buttons / 20 px artboard, 200 ms cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0.38,0.9) hover, no bounce, no overshoot.
  • Reduced-motion gateSkeleton shimmer + card-reveal stagger respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (1 ms duration override). No theme toggle UI rendered anywhere — light theme only per the iron rule.
