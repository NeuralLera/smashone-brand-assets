From application to first payout

Four steps. Eight minutes to apply.

Stripe Connect Express handles bank linking, KYC and 1099-NEC tax forms. You focus on customers — we handle the payout plumbing.

1

Apply

Eight-minute form. Name, email, US legal residence, age 18+ confirmation, automator role intent. We review within 3 business days based on track record and fit.

2

Link bank

Stripe Connect Express OAuth flow. Link your bank account, provide SSN or EIN, complete KYC. Stripe handles all sensitive data — we never see your full SSN.

3

KYC + tax setup

W-9 collected automatically. 1099-NEC auto-issued each January for earnings ≥ $600 / year. Compliance handled — no manual paperwork on your end.

4

Get your referral link

Custom subdomain yourname.smashone.us + UTM-tagged tracking links. Dashboard tracks every click, signup, install and payout in real time.

First payout typically lands ~Day 30 of your first install (after the 14-day money-back window). Monthly subscription partnership payouts start Month 2 of customer lifetime, via ACH direct deposit.

Partnership math

How L1 + L2 subscription partnership works.

Two commission levels only. No level 3+. No team-building bonuses. No volume thresholds. Not MLM.

Partnership math

L1 Direct partnership

$7.90 / mo per active client

10% of $79 / mo subscription. Lifetime — stops the month the customer cancels.

L2 Indirect partnership

$3.95 / mo per L2 client

5% of $79 / mo subscription. Lifetime — stops the month the L2 customer cancels.

Hard caps

  • Only 2 levels — no L3 / L4 / unlimited downline
  • No qualifying volumes or team-building bonuses
  • No clawbacks past Day 30

This is not MLM. There are exactly two commission levels. You can never owe SmashOne money. Each commission is a percentage of an actual recurring subscription payment — no «product purchases» required.
