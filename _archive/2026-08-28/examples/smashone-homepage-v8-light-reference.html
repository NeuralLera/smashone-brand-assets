Mary opens High Cotton Antiques at 10 AM. By afternoon, Instagram DMs pile up — "Is the Eastlake table still available?" "Do you deliver to Charlotte?" — and Mary can't stop to answer while she's helping a customer in the showroom.

With SmashOne, daily new-arrival posts publish to Facebook, Instagram, and Telegram at the same hour every morning — no thinking required. Her catalog shows all 134 items with photos, provenance, dimensions, and prices. Customers reserve from any platform.

Now Mary focuses on the showroom floor. The phone is quieter. The bell rings more. She's closing at 5 PM most days — first time since she inherited the shop three years ago.
